In short: X-Ray Camera renders interactive 3D anatomical visualizations on your device. It has no accounts and no ads, and I never receive your camera images or tracking data. It has no internet permission, so nothing it does can leave your device on its own. If you choose to use my Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy. Camera and Tracking Data X-Ray Camera uses the camera permission to capture live video frames in real time, detect your hand pose, and project the 3D anatomical X-ray model over your hand. Camera frames and hand landmarks are processed strictly in memory on your device and are never recorded, saved as files, or uploaded to any server. Your camera data never leaves your device. Information That Stays on Your Device Your app settings (display mode, bone intensity, rendering preferences) and hand calibration measurements are stored locally in the app's private storage. I never receive them. If you explicitly turn on "Record hand points" in settings for debug tracing, spatial landmark coordinates (2D/3D numerical points, never camera images) are stored in private app storage until you delete them or uninstall the app. Information That Leaves Your Device X-Ray Camera does not request the internet permission and does not send any data to me or to anyone else. It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or any third-party service that collects data, and it does not read the Android advertising ID. Backups Android backup is turned off for the app. Your settings, calibration, and traces are never included in a backup to your Google account or in a device-to-device transfer. Permissions X-Ray Camera Asks For Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): to access the camera sensor, track hand pose movement in real time, and render the anatomical X-ray model over your hand. Sharing I do not sell, rent or share your data. No data is shared with advertisers or data brokers. I may disclose information if the law requires it, but the app gives me nothing to disclose. Retention and Your Choices Your settings, calibration and recorded traces stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app; uninstalling removes them all. You can revoke the camera permission in Android settings at any time. If you email me, I keep the conversation only as long as needed to answer you. Ask me and I will delete it. Security Your data is processed only on your device. But remember that no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure and reliable, and I cannot guarantee its absolute security. Children's Privacy These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. The app asks for no account or personal details. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact me so that I will be able to do necessary actions.

Privacy Policy Stanislav Georgiev ("I", "me") built the Files app (com.slaviboy.myfiles) as a free app. This SERVICE is provided by me at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform visitors regarding my policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information if anyone decided to use my Service. In short: Files is a file manager. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and I never receive your files. Your files leave your device only when you send them somewhere yourself. The only thing the app sends to me is crash reports, which you can turn off in Settings. If you choose to use my Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy. Your Files Files reads and changes files only to show and manage them when you ask it to, on this device and on storage you connect. If you allow All files access, it is used only for that. File names, contents and folder listings are not collected, uploaded or shared by me. Your files leave your device only when you send them somewhere yourself, for example by copying them to a server you added or sharing them with another app. What that server or app does with them is under its own terms. Crash Reports If the app crashes, it sends a report through Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service, so the problem can be found and fixed. A report contains where in the app's code the crash happened, the app and Android versions, the device model, some device state such as free memory, and random installation IDs. Error messages are removed before sending, so the names and contents of your files are never included. Reports are kept for up to 90 days. You can turn them off at any time in Settings > Privacy > Send crash reports; reports not sent yet are then deleted. The app does not read the Android advertising ID. Firebase Crashlytics: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy Servers You Add The app connects only to the servers you add (SMB, SFTP, FTP, WebDAV and HTTP). Their addresses and settings stay on your device. Passwords, keys and tokens you choose to remember are encrypted with a key held in Android's keystore, are sent only to the server they belong to, and are never included in backups. Looking for servers nearby sends a discovery request on your local network only. Some servers use plain, unencrypted connections; the app warns you before it saves one. Cloud Storage Cloud folders are opened through the cloud provider's own app and Android's file picker. The cloud app manages your account; Files never sees your cloud sign-in details. Voice Search Voice search, only when you tap the microphone button, uses the speech recognition service on your device to turn your speech into text. That service, not Files, handles the audio, under its provider's privacy terms. Files keeps only the resulting search text in your recent searches, which you can clear. The sound is never recorded or stored by the app. App Lock and Secure Folder Your PIN or password is stored on your device only as a salted hash. Fingerprint and face unlock are handled by Android; Files never receives biometric data. Files in the Secure folder are encrypted on your device with keys held in Android's keystore, and are lost for good if the app's data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Backups Settings backups you create are saved where you choose and never contain passwords, keys or tokens. If Android backup is on, Android may include the app's settings (such as theme and layout) in your device backup to your Google account. This is managed by Android and Google under your backup settings and Google's privacy policy; I have no access to it. Your files, favorites, servers, saved passwords, app lock and Secure folder are never part of it. Permissions Files Asks For Each permission is asked for only when you use a feature that needs it: All files access and media access (photos, videos, audio): to show and manage the files on your device. This is the core purpose of a file manager. Microphone: for voice search, only when you tap the microphone button. Notifications: to show the progress of long operations such as copying. Internet and local network access: to reach the servers you add and to find servers on your network. Install apps: to hand an APK you open to the system installer; Android asks you to confirm every install. Biometrics: for app lock. Sharing I do not sell, rent or share your data. The only data that reaches me or a service is the crash reports described above, which are processed by Google (Firebase Crashlytics) on my behalf. No data is shared with advertisers or data brokers. I may disclose information if the law requires it, but the app gives me nothing besides crash reports to disclose. Retention and Your Choices Your settings, favorites, servers and other app data stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app; uninstalling removes them all. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days, and you can turn them off at any time. You can revoke any permission in Android settings at any time. If you email me, I keep the conversation only as long as needed to answer you. Ask me and I will delete it. Security Passwords and keys you remember are encrypted with Android's keystore, and Secure folder files are encrypted on your device. But remember that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and I cannot guarantee its absolute security. Children's Privacy These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. The app asks for no account or personal details. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact me so that I will be able to do necessary actions. Your Rights Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data, or to object to it. Crash reports use random installation IDs and contain no name, email or file content; if you want one deleted or have emailed me, contact me to exercise a right. Changes to This Privacy Policy I may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

Privacy Policy Stanislav Georgiev ("I", "me") built the SkyMap app (com.slaviboy.skymap) as a free app. This SERVICE is provided by me at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform visitors regarding my policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information if anyone decided to use my Service. In short: SkyMap does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your location and the camera picture are used on your device only, to show the sky where you are and where you point your phone, and are never sent to me or to anyone else. The app goes online only to download its sky and planet data from my repository on GitHub and to load articles and pictures from Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons and NASA, and, while Send crash reports is on, to send crash reports, which you can turn off in Settings. If you choose to use my Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy. Information That Stays on Your Device Location. If you allow the approximate location permission, SkyMap reads your position when you ask it to find your observing site, so it can work out which stars and planets are above you and when. It is used on your device, and the place you choose (found by the app or picked by you) is kept in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded or shared by me. Without the permission the app works as well: you pick a place yourself. Camera. If you allow the camera permission, the AR view shows the live picture from your camera behind the sky. The picture is shown on the screen only. It is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere. SkyMap takes no photos or videos. Motion sensors. The compass, gyroscope and similar sensors are read to know where your phone points. This stays in the app's memory and is never stored or sent. Your choices and lists. Your settings, favourite objects, observing lists, quiz progress and similar options are stored in the app's private storage. I never receive them. Information That Leaves Your Device SkyMap asks for the internet permission only for the following. None of it carries your location, your camera picture, your favourites or any other personal data. Sky and planet data. The star catalogue, maps and pictures of the Moon and planets, and similar data packs are downloaded from my repository on GitHub (github.com/slaviboy/skymap-data) and kept on your device for offline use. As with any download, GitHub can see your IP address and which file is asked for, and handles that under its own terms. Articles and pictures. When you open an object's page, its description and picture may be loaded from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons (in your app language), and live images of the Sun from NASA's Solar Dynamics Observatory. These services can see your IP address, your device type and which article or picture is asked for, and handle that under their own terms. Crash reports. While Settings > Send crash reports is on, SkyMap sends crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service, so I can find and fix what fails. It is on when you install the app and you can turn it off at any time; turning it off stops collection and deletes reports not sent yet. Debug builds never send them. A crash report contains which error happened and where in the app's code, the app and Android versions, the device model, some device state such as free memory, and a random ID for this installation. It never contains your location, camera picture, favourites or any other content of yours. Google Play services. To find your position more quickly, the app asks Google's location service on your device, when it is there, and the system's own location providers otherwise. That is handled by Google and Android under their own terms; the position goes to the app, not to me. Android backup. If you have turned on backup in your Android settings, Android may include SkyMap's settings in your device backup to your Google account. This is managed by Android and Google under your backup settings and Google's privacy policy; I have no access to it. Other actions you start yourself hand over to another app: Contact opens your email app with a new message to me (nothing is sent until you send it yourself, and then I receive only what you write and your email address, which I use only to answer you); Share opens Android's share sheet; links open in your browser under their own privacy policies. Optional; asked only when you ask the app to find your location. Camera: to show the live picture behind the sky in the AR view. Optional; asked only when you open that view. Internet and network state: to download the data and articles described above, and to know whether a connection is there. Sharing I do not collect, sell, rent or share any personal data. No data is shared with advertisers, data brokers or any other third party. The only third parties that receive anything are those named above (GitHub, Wikipedia and Wikimedia, NASA, and Google for crash reports), and only what is described above. I may disclose information if the law requires it, but the app gives me none to disclose beyond crash reports. Retention and Your Choices Your settings, places and lists stay on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app; uninstalling removes them all. Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for a limited time under Google's own retention rules. You can turn them off at any time in Settings. You can revoke the location or camera permission in Android settings at any time. If you email me, I keep the conversation only as long as needed to answer you. Ask me and I will delete it. Security Your settings are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device, and everything the app downloads is fetched over HTTPS. But remember that no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure and reliable, and I cannot guarantee its absolute security. Children's Privacy These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. The app asks for no account or personal details. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact me so that I will be able to do necessary actions. Your Rights Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data, or to object to it. SkyMap holds none of yours on my side; if you have emailed me, contact me to exercise a right over that conversation. Crash reports carry only a random installation ID and no name or contact detail, so I cannot tie them to you. Changes to This Privacy Policy I may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. I will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

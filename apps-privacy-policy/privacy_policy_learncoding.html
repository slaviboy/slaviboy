Privacy Policy Stanislav Georgiev ("I", "me") built the LearnCoding app (com.slaviboy.learncoding) as a free app. This SERVICE is provided by me at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform visitors regarding my policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information if anyone decided to use my Service. In short: in LearnCoding your progress, the code you write and your settings stay on your device, and I do not run any account system or server that stores your personal data. The only data that leaves your device is an optional crash report and the downloads of the course content and of the code runtime. If you choose to use my Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy. Information That Stays on Your Device Your learning data. Your progress, quiz answers, the code you write and run, and your settings are stored in the app's private storage. I never receive them. Code you run. Programs you write in the playground are compiled and run on your device. The code and its output are not uploaded. The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location or photos, and does not show notifications. Information That Leaves Your Device Crash reports (optional) Release builds of LearnCoding use Firebase Crashlytics, a service of Google LLC, to find and fix crashes. This is on by default and you can turn it off at any time in LearnCoding > Settings > Send crash reports. Debug builds never send reports. The data collected is: App info and performance: crash logs and diagnostics such as the app version, Android version, device model and the state of the app when the error happened. Purpose: fix crashes. Device or other IDs: a random Firebase installation ID. Google may process your IP address to deliver the data. Purpose: tell installations apart and group crashes without identifying you. A crash report never contains your lessons, code, answers or progress. LearnCoding does not read the Android advertising ID, does not use advertising or ad personalisation, does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and does not sell your data. Google processes this data on my behalf as a service provider. The data is encrypted in transit. Link to privacy policy of third party service providers used by the app Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy Course downloads The course (lessons, quizzes and examples) is downloaded from GitHub, where it is published by me. The app checks for updates once a day while you are online. Like any web request, GitHub can see your IP address and the request time under its own privacy policy. I do not add any identifier of yours to the request, and I do not receive your IP address. GitHub: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement Code runtime download The tools that compile and run your code are delivered by Google Play as an on-demand feature module when you first need them. Your device requests it from Google Play, which can see your IP address and the request time under its own privacy policy. Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy Links to other websites open in your browser only when you tap them, and then the privacy policy of that website applies. Permissions LearnCoding Asks For Internet and network state: download the course and the code runtime, send optional crash reports, and show whether something failed because you are offline. No other permissions are requested. Sharing I do not sell or rent data and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The only parties that receive data are Google (Firebase Crashlytics and Google Play) and GitHub, as described above. I may disclose information if the law requires it. Retention and Your Choices On-device content stays until you delete it or uninstall the app; uninstalling removes it all. Crash data is kept by Firebase for the period set by Google's defaults, after which it is deleted automatically. You can switch off Send crash reports at any time. Because the data is tied to a random installation ID and not to your identity, I cannot look up one person's data; if you want your installation's data removed, contact me and describe the request. Security Data in transit is sent over HTTPS. Your progress and code are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and I cannot guarantee its absolute security.

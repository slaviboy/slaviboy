Privacy Policy Stanislav Georgiev ("I", "me") built the LearnChemistry app (com.slaviboy.chemistry) as a free app. This SERVICE is provided by me at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform visitors regarding my policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information if anyone decided to use my Service. In short: in LearnChemistry your progress, answers, drawings and settings stay on your device, and I do not run any account system or server that stores your personal data. The only data that leaves your device is an optional crash report, the searches you start yourself, and the downloads of the handwriting model. If you choose to use my Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy. Information That Stays on Your Device Your learning data. Your progress, practice answers, drawn structures, history and settings are stored in the app's private storage. I never receive them. Camera. If you allow the camera permission, LearnChemistry uses it only when you open the scanner to read a chemistry problem. The picture is processed on your device (text recognition runs on the device) and is not uploaded. You can also pick a photo from your gallery or type the problem instead. Photos you choose. LearnChemistry opens an image only through the system picker, and only the one you pick. Handwriting. Drawings and handwriting are recognised on your device. The strokes are not uploaded. Notifications. If you allow the notification permission, the app may show practice reminders. They are created on your device. Information That Leaves Your Device Crash reports (optional) Release builds of LearnChemistry use Firebase Crashlytics, a service of Google LLC, to find and fix crashes. This is on by default and you can turn it off at any time in LearnChemistry > Settings > Send crash reports. Debug builds never send reports. The data collected is: App info and performance: crash logs and diagnostics such as the app version, Android version, device model and the state of the app when the error happened. Purpose: fix crashes. Device or other IDs: a random Firebase installation ID. Google may process your IP address to deliver the data. Purpose: tell installations apart and group crashes without identifying you. A crash report never contains your answers, drawings, photos or name. LearnChemistry does not read the Android advertising ID, does not use advertising or ad personalisation, does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and does not sell your data. Google processes this data on my behalf as a service provider. The data is encrypted in transit. Link to privacy policy of third party service providers used by the app Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy Google ML Kit: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure Searches you start When you search for a molecule or compound, the text of your search is sent to PubChem (US National Library of Medicine, NCBI) to get the result. This happens only for searches you start. Like any web request, the host can see your IP address and the request time under its own privacy policy. I do not add any identifier of yours to the request. Links in the periodic table to Wikipedia, Wikidata, PubChem and NIST open in your browser only when you tap them, and then the privacy policy of that website applies. PubChem: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/ Model downloads The handwriting and text recognition models are provided by Google ML Kit. The handwriting model is downloaded the first time you use it. Your device requests it from Google, which can see your IP address and the request time under its own privacy policy. ML Kit does not send your handwriting or camera images to Google; they are recognised on your device. Permissions LearnChemistry Asks For Internet and network state: search compounds, download the handwriting model, send optional crash reports, show whether something failed because you are offline. Camera: scan a chemistry problem. Requested only when you open the scanner. Notifications: practice reminders. Requested only when you turn them on. Sharing I do not sell or rent data and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The only parties that receive data are Google (Firebase Crashlytics and ML Kit model download) and PubChem, as described above. I may disclose information if the law requires it. Retention and Your Choices On-device content stays until you delete it or uninstall the app; uninstalling removes it all. Crash data is kept by Firebase for the period set by Google's defaults, after which it is deleted automatically. You can switch off Send crash reports at any time. Because the data is tied to a random installation ID and not to your identity, I cannot look up one person's data; if you want your installation's data removed, contact me and describe the request. You can revoke the camera and notification permissions in Android settings at any time. Security Data in transit is sent over HTTPS. Your progress and drawings are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and I cannot guarantee its absolute security. Children's Privacy These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. The app asks for no account or personal details. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact me so that I will be able to do necessary actions. Your Rights Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data, or to object to it. Crash data is collected on the basis of your choice (the Send crash reports switch) and can be withdrawn at any time. Contact me to exercise a right.

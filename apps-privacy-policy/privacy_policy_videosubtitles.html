Privacy Policy Stanislav Georgiev ("I", "me") built the VideoSubtitles app (com.slaviboy.videosubtitles) as a free app. This SERVICE is provided by me at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform visitors regarding my policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information if anyone decided to use my Service. In short: VideoSubtitles edits your videos and makes their subtitles on your device. Speech is recognised on the device, and I never receive your videos, audio, pictures or subtitles. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It goes online only to download the speech and translation models you choose and to send crash reports, which you can turn off in Settings. If you choose to use my Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy. Your Media, Projects and Subtitles VideoSubtitles opens the videos, sounds and pictures you choose with Android's file picker. It reads them where they are; when the app that provides a file does not allow lasting access to it, a copy is kept in the app's private storage. The sound is extracted, turned into text, edited, translated and styled on your device, and your projects, subtitles and settings are saved in the app's private storage. Videos you export are saved to your gallery (Movies/VideoSubtitles) or where you choose, and SRT and WebVTT subtitle files where you choose. Your media, the text of your subtitles and the names of your files and projects are not collected, uploaded or shared by me. They leave your device only when you send them somewhere yourself, for example by sharing an exported video or subtitle file with another app. What that app does with them is under its own terms. Speech Recognition Speech is turned into text by speech recognition models that run on your device. Your audio is never sent to me or to anyone else to be recognised. Translation Translation models downloaded from their publishers translate your subtitles on your device. If you choose to translate with ML Kit, a Google service, the translation also happens on your device, and the text you translate is not sent to Google. ML Kit downloads its language models from Google, and it sends Google information about how its features perform, such as the device model, the app and Android versions and performance figures, as Google describes here: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure Downloading Models The app downloads the list of available models and the models you choose directly from their publishers: Hugging Face (huggingface.co), Alpha Cephei (alphacephei.com), Mozilla (cdn.mozilla.net) and GitHub (github.com), which also holds my own copies of the models for when a publisher cannot be reached. As with any download, the server you connect to can see your IP address and the request, and handles it under its own terms. Nothing about your media, subtitles or projects is sent with a download. Crash Reports If Send crash reports is on and the app crashes, it sends a report through Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service, so the crash can be found and fixed. A report contains the error and where in the app's code it happened, the app and Android versions, the device model, some device state such as free memory and storage, and a random installation ID. It never contains your videos, audio, pictures or subtitles; an error message can, rarely, include the name of a file the app was working on. Reports are kept for up to 90 days. You can turn them off at any time in Settings > Privacy > Send crash reports; reports not sent yet are then deleted. Firebase Crashlytics: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy Reporting a Bug and Sharing the App Report a bug in Settings opens your email app with the app version and device details filled in; nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. The QR code for sharing the app is made on your device and contains only the link to the app's page on Google Play. Backups If Android backup is on for your device, your projects (their names, subtitles and edits, and references to the media files they use) and the app's settings can be included in your backup to your Google account and in a transfer to a new device, which Google stores under its own terms. Your videos, sounds, pictures and the downloaded models are never included. You can turn backup off in Android settings. Permissions VideoSubtitles Asks For Files are picked with Android's file picker, so VideoSubtitles does not need access to all your files, photos, videos or storage. It does not use the microphone, camera, contacts or location. Internet and network state: to download the models you choose and to send the crash reports described above. Notifications: to show the progress of downloads, transcription, translation and export. Foreground service, wake lock and run at startup: to keep that work running while the app is in the background, and to finish it after the phone restarts. Sharing I do not sell, rent or share your data. The only data that reaches me is the crash reports described above, which are processed by Google (Firebase) on my behalf. No data is shared with advertisers or data brokers. I may disclose information if the law requires it, but the app gives me nothing besides those crash reports to disclose. Retention and Your Choices Your media copies, projects, subtitles, downloaded models and settings stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app; uninstalling removes them all. Files you exported to your gallery or elsewhere stay there. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. You can turn off crash reports at any time in Settings, and remove downloaded models in Settings > Speech & translation models. You can revoke any permission in Android settings at any time. If you email me, I keep the conversation only as long as needed to answer you. Ask me and I will delete it. Security Your media and subtitles are processed only on your device, and models and crash reports travel over encrypted connections. But remember that no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure and reliable, and I cannot guarantee its absolute security. Children's Privacy These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. The app asks for no account or personal details. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact me so that I will be able to do necessary actions. Your Rights Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data, or to object to it. Crash reports use a random installation ID and contain no name, email or media; if you want one deleted or have emailed me, contact me to exercise a right.

Privacy Policy Stanislav Georgiev ("I", "me") built the OfficePro app (com.slaviboy.officepro) as a paid app. This SERVICE is provided by me and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform visitors regarding my policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information if anyone decided to use my Service. In short: OfficePro opens, edits and creates documents, spreadsheets, presentations and PDFs on your device, and I never receive your files or what they contain. The app has no accounts and no ads, and it reads no advertising ID. It goes online only to download fonts and licence texts from my own repositories and, while Share usage statistics is on, to send usage statistics and crash reports, which you can turn off in Settings. If you choose to use my Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy. Your Documents OfficePro opens the files you choose: from its own library, from folders you add with Android's folder picker (phone storage, an SD card or a cloud app's folder), with Android's file picker, or when another app opens or shares a file with OfficePro. Your documents are read, edited, converted, printed and saved on your device. New files, your templates and the version history of your files are kept in the app's private storage, and so are the copies OfficePro needs to work: copies of files whose app does not allow lasting access, files you keep for offline use, and snapshots of unsaved changes, so they can be brought back after a crash. The text, pictures, names and paths of your files are not collected, uploaded or shared by me. They leave your device only when you send them somewhere yourself, for example by sharing, exporting or printing a file, or by saving it to a cloud app's folder. What that app does with them is under its own terms. Passwords and Signatures Passwords you give a document are used on your device to open or protect it, and are never stored or sent anywhere. Keys you create or import to sign PDFs are kept in Android's Keystore on your device; certificates you pick from Android's own list stay where Android keeps them. Signatures are checked on your device, without an online check. Features That Use Your Device's Own Services Some features hand work to services of your device, which handle it under their own terms: Spelling uses your device's spell checker, such as your keyboard's. Read aloud uses your device's text-to-speech engine. Insert a picture from the camera and Scan to PDF use your device's camera app; OfficePro itself has no camera permission. Print uses Android's print service and the printer you choose. Recognise Text (OCR) runs on your device; the pages are not sent anywhere. Fonts and Licence Texts When a document uses a font that is not on your device, or you pick one from the Font list, OfficePro downloads it from my own font repository on GitHub (github.com/slaviboy/FreeFonts), or from its copy on GitLab (gitlab.com/slaviboy/FreeFonts) when GitHub cannot be reached, and keeps it for offline use. Settings > Open-source licenses loads the current licence texts from my repository on GitHub (github.com/slaviboy/slaviboy), and shows the copies inside the app when there is no connection. As with any download, GitHub or GitLab can see your IP address and which file is asked for, and handle that under their own terms. Nothing about your documents is sent with a download, only the name of the font. Usage Statistics and Crash Reports While Settings > Privacy > Share usage statistics is on, OfficePro sends usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase and crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics, both Google services, so I can see which features are used and find and fix what fails. It is on when you install the app, and you can turn it off at any time; turning it off stops both at once and deletes crash reports not sent yet. Debug builds never send either. Usage statistics say what was done with which kind of file, never which file: a document opened (its format, such as DOCX or PDF, where it was opened from, and whether for viewing or editing), a document that could not be opened and why, a document created (its format and which built-in template, if any), saved, saved in another format, exported, shared or printed, and the use of a few features, such as Replace All, Read aloud, a slideshow or setting a password. They never contain a file's name, path or content, or anything you type. Google Analytics also records, as it does for every app that uses it, a random ID for this installation, when the app was first opened and how long it is used, the app and Android versions, the device model and language, the country or region your IP address points to, and, for an app installed from Google Play, how it was found there (the install referrer). A crash report contains which error happened and where in the app's code, the app and Android versions, the device model, some device state such as free memory and storage, the same random installation ID, and the kind of document that was open, such as DOCX. OfficePro also reports some failures the app recovers from, such as a file it could not read; those reports carry only the error's name and where in the code it happened, never its message, which could name a file. A report never contains your files or their content. Google Analytics keeps this event data for up to 14 months, and Crashlytics keeps crash reports for up to 90 days. Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy Reporting a Bug and Sharing the App Report a bug in Settings opens your email app with the app version and device details filled in; nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. OfficePro also keeps a small log of what it did (events and errors, never the content of your files) in its private storage, to help with a bug report; it never leaves your device unless you send it yourself. The QR code for sharing the app is part of the app and contains only the link to its page on Google Play. Backups If Android backup is on for your device, OfficePro's settings can be included in your backup to your Google account, which Google stores under its own terms. Your documents are never included in that backup. When you move to a new device with Android's device-to-device transfer, which you start yourself, your OfficePro library and its list of recent and favorite files come along too. Version history, the copies of other apps' files and the snapshots of unsaved changes never leave the device, so no earlier or unprotected copy of a password-protected document is ever backed up. You can turn backup off in Android settings. Permissions OfficePro Asks For Files are picked with Android's file and folder pickers, so OfficePro does not need access to all your files, photos or storage. It does not use the microphone, camera, contacts or location. Internet and network state: to download fonts and licence texts and to send the usage statistics and crash reports described above. Foreground service, wake lock and run at startup: used by Android's WorkManager to tidy the app's private storage and to keep files kept for offline use up to date, also after the phone restarts. Install referrer: used by Google Analytics to tell how the app was found on Google Play. Sharing I do not sell, rent or share your data. The only data that reaches me is the usage statistics and crash reports described above, which are processed by Google (Firebase) on my behalf. No data is shared with advertisers or data brokers. I may disclose information if the law requires it, but the app gives me nothing besides those statistics and reports to disclose. Retention and Your Choices Your files, templates, version history, downloaded fonts and settings stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app; uninstalling removes everything in the app's private storage. Files in folders you added, or that you saved or exported elsewhere, stay there. Usage statistics are kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days. You can turn off usage statistics and crash reports at any time in Settings > Privacy > Share usage statistics, and clear temporary files in Settings > Storage. If you email me, I keep the conversation only as long as needed to answer you. Ask me and I will delete it. Security Your documents are processed only on your device, and fonts, licence texts, statistics and reports travel over encrypted connections. But remember that no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure and reliable, and I cannot guarantee its absolute security. Children's Privacy These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. The app asks for no account or personal details. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact me so that I will be able to do necessary actions. Usage statistics and crash reports use a random installation ID and contain no name, email or document; to object to them, turn off Share usage statistics.

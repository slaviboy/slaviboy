This page is used to inform visitors regarding my policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information if anyone decided to use my Service. In short: Guitar Tuner collects no personal data. It has no internet permission, so nothing it does can leave your device on its own. Sound from the microphone is analysed in memory to find the pitch and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. If you choose to use my Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy. Information That Stays on Your Device Microphone. If you allow the microphone permission, Guitar Tuner uses it only while the tuner is listening. The sound is analysed on your device, in real time, to measure the pitch of your instrument. It is processed in memory and discarded; it is never recorded, saved to a file or uploaded. Settings. Your choices (instrument and tuning, reference pitch, theme and colours, haptics, metronome tempo and similar options) are stored in the app's private storage. I never receive them. Information That Leaves Your Device Guitar Tuner does not request the internet permission and does not send any data to me or to anyone else. It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or any other third-party service that collects data, and it does not read the Android advertising ID. The only ways anything leaves your device are actions you start yourself, which hand over to another app: Contact opens your email app with a new message to me; nothing is sent until you send it yourself, and then I receive only what you write and your email address, which I use only to answer you. The GitHub link opens in your browser, under its own privacy policy. Android backup. If you have turned on backup in your Android settings, Android may include Guitar Tuner's settings in your device backup to your Google account. This is managed by Android and Google under your backup settings and Google's privacy policy; I have no access to it.

Privacy Policy Stanislav Georgiev ("I", "me") built the ConvertAll app (com.slaviboy.convertall) as a free app. This SERVICE is provided by me at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform visitors regarding my policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information if anyone decided to use my Service. In short: ConvertAll converts your files on your device. It has no accounts and no ads, and I never receive your files. The only things the app can send to me are anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, and you can turn both off in Settings. If you choose to use my Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy. Your Files ConvertAll reads the files you choose, converts them on your device, and saves the results where you ask. Conversions never use the network. File names, contents, paths and the converted results are not collected, uploaded or shared by me. Your files leave your device only when you send them somewhere yourself, for example by sharing a converted file with another app. What that app does with them is under its own terms. Imported files and converted results are kept in the app's private storage until you delete them or uninstall the app. Usage Statistics If Share usage statistics is on, the app sends anonymous usage statistics through Firebase Analytics, a Google service, so I can see which conversions are used and which fail. Only these events are sent: a file was imported, a conversion started, completed, failed or was cancelled, and a converted file was saved. They carry format names, categories, counts, rough size and duration ranges and error codes. Firebase's automatic events (such as first open and session start), the app and Android versions, the device model, the country and language, and a random app installation ID are included too. Nothing is logged for the screens you visit, and file names, paths, links, text you type and file contents are never sent. The app does not read the Android advertising ID or the Android ID, and does not use the data for ads. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Privacy > Share usage statistics; the app then stops sending and resets its analytics data on your device. Crash Reports If Send crash reports is on and the app crashes, or an unexpected conversion error happens, it sends a report through Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service, so the problem can be found and fixed. A report contains where in the app's code the problem happened, the format and conversion type involved, an error code, the app and Android versions, the device model, some device state such as free memory, and a random installation ID. Error messages are removed before sending, so the names and contents of your files are never included. Reports are kept for up to 90 days. You can turn them off at any time in Settings > Privacy > Send crash reports; reports not sent yet are then deleted. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy Downloads From a Link Only when you choose to add a file from a link and start the download, the app connects to the address you entered to download that file. The server you connect to can see your IP address and the request, as with any download, and handles it under its own terms. The address you enter stays on your device and is never sent to me or to Firebase. Backups Android backup is turned off for the app. Your files, conversion history and settings are never included in a backup to your Google account or in a device-to-device transfer. Permissions ConvertAll Asks For Files are picked with Android's file picker or received when you share a file to the app, so ConvertAll does not need access to all your files, photos or storage. Internet and network state: to download a file from a link you add, and to send the usage statistics and crash reports described above. Notifications: to show the progress of a conversion that is running. Foreground service: to keep a conversion running while the app is in the background. Sharing I do not sell, rent or share your data. The only data that reaches me or a service is the usage statistics and crash reports described above, which are processed by Google (Firebase) on my behalf. No data is shared with advertisers or data brokers. I may disclose information if the law requires it, but the app gives me nothing besides those statistics and crash reports to disclose. Retention and Your Choices Your files, conversion history and settings stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app; uninstalling removes them all. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. Usage statistics are kept by Firebase Analytics for no more than 14 months. You can turn off usage statistics and crash reports at any time in Settings. You can revoke any permission in Android settings at any time. If you email me, I keep the conversation only as long as needed to answer you. Ask me and I will delete it. Security Your files are processed only on your device, and data sent to Firebase is sent over an encrypted connection. But remember that no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure and reliable, and I cannot guarantee its absolute security. Children's Privacy These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. The app asks for no account or personal details. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact me so that I will be able to do necessary actions. Your Rights Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal data, or to object to it. Usage statistics and crash reports use random installation IDs and contain no name, email or file content; if you want one deleted or have emailed me, contact me to exercise a right.

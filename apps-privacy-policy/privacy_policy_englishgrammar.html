Privacy Policy Stanislav Georgiev ("I", "me") built the Grammar Academy app (com.slaviboy.englishgrammar) as a free app. This SERVICE is provided by me at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform visitors regarding my policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information if anyone decided to use my Service. In short: in Grammar Academy your progress, answers, handwriting, notes and settings stay on your device, and I do not run any account system or server that stores your personal data. The only data that leaves your device is an optional crash report and the download of the handwriting model. If you choose to use my Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy. Information That Stays on Your Device Your learning data. Your progress, practice answers, notes, history and settings are stored in the app's private storage. I never receive them. Handwriting. Handwriting is recognised on your device. The strokes are not uploaded. Notifications. If you turn on the daily reminder and allow the notification permission, the app shows a study reminder. It is created on your device. Information That Leaves Your Device Crash reports (optional) Release builds of Grammar Academy use Firebase Crashlytics, a service of Google LLC, to find and fix crashes. This is on by default and you can turn it off at any time in Grammar Academy > Settings > Privacy > Crash reports. Debug builds never send reports. The data collected is: App info and performance: crash logs and diagnostics such as the app version, Android version, device model and the state of the app when the error happened. Purpose: fix crashes. Device or other IDs: a random Firebase installation ID. Google may process your IP address to deliver the data. Purpose: tell installations apart and group crashes without identifying you. A crash report never contains your answers, handwriting, notes or name. Grammar Academy does not read the Android advertising ID, does not use advertising or ad personalisation, does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and does not sell your data. Google processes this data on my behalf as a service provider. The data is encrypted in transit. Link to privacy policy of third party service providers used by the app Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy Google ML Kit: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure Model downloads The handwriting recognition model is provided by Google ML Kit. It is downloaded the first time you use handwriting. Your device requests it from Google, which can see your IP address and the request time under its own privacy policy. ML Kit does not send your handwriting to Google; it is recognised on your device. Permissions Grammar Academy Asks For Internet and network state: send optional crash reports, download the handwriting model, and know whether you are offline. Notifications: the daily study reminder. Requested only when you turn it on. Wake lock, run at startup and foreground service: used by the system scheduler so the reminder and background model download can run on time. They do not collect any data. Sharing I do not sell or rent data and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The only party that receives data is Google (Firebase Crashlytics and ML Kit model download), as described above. I may disclose information if the law requires it. Retention and Your Choices On-device content stays until you delete it or uninstall the app; uninstalling removes it all. Crash data is kept by Firebase for the period set by Google's defaults, after which it is deleted automatically. You can switch off Crash reports at any time. Because the data is tied to a random installation ID and not to your identity, I cannot look up one person's data; if you want your installation's data removed, contact me and describe the request. You can revoke the notification permission in Android settings at any time. Security Data in transit is sent over HTTPS. Your progress and handwriting are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and I cannot guarantee its absolute security. Children's Privacy These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. The app asks for no account or personal details. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact me so that I will be able to do necessary actions.

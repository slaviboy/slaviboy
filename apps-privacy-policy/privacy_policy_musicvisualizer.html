In short: MusicVisualizer collects no personal data. It has no internet permission, so nothing it does can leave your device on its own. Sound from the microphone is turned into light on your device and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. If you choose to use my Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy. Information That Stays on Your Device Microphone. If you allow the microphone permission, MusicVisualizer uses it only after you press Start microphone, and stops it completely when you press stop. The sound is analyzed on your device, in real time, to drive the visuals. It is never recorded, saved to a file or uploaded. You can also use the app without the microphone by playing the built-in demo track, which ships inside the app. Settings and saved looks. Your settings, the language you pick and the looks (presets) you save are stored in the app's private storage. I never receive them. Files you choose. MusicVisualizer opens or saves preset files only through the system file picker, and only the files you pick. Where a file goes after you export it (another app, email, cloud storage) is up to you and that app. Information That Leaves Your Device MusicVisualizer does not request the internet permission and does not send any data to me or to anyone else. It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or any other third-party service that collects data, and it does not read the Android advertising ID. The only ways anything leaves your device are actions you start yourself, which hand over to another app: Share link opens the Android share sheet with the app's Google Play link, for the app you choose to share it with. Report a bug opens your email app with a new message to me; nothing is sent until you send it yourself, and then I receive only what you write and your email address, which I use only to answer you. GitHub and the Google Play link open in your browser or the Play Store app, under their own privacy policies. Android backup. If you have turned on backup in your Android settings, Android may include MusicVisualizer's settings and saved looks in your device backup to your Google account. This is managed by Android and Google under your backup settings and Google's privacy policy; I have no access to it.

Privacy Policy Stanislav Georgiev ("I", "me") built the Driving Theory Test app (com.slaviboy.drivingtests) as a free app. This SERVICE is provided by me at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform visitors regarding my policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information if anyone decided to use my Service. In short: in Driving Theory Test your progress, bookmarks, test results and settings stay on your device, and I do not run any account system or server that stores your personal data. The only data that leaves your device is an optional crash report and the downloads of new question releases and videos. If you choose to use my Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy. Information That Stays on Your Device Your learning data. Your progress, answers, bookmarks, test results and settings are stored in the app's private storage. I never receive them. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this data in your Google backup under Google's own privacy policy. The app needs no account, name, email address, phone number, location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos, and it asks for none of these permissions. Information That Leaves Your Device Crash reports (optional) Release builds of Driving Theory Test use Firebase Crashlytics, a service of Google LLC, to find and fix crashes. This is on by default and you can turn it off at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy. Debug builds never send reports. The data collected is: App info and performance: crash logs and diagnostics such as the app version, Android version, device model and the state of the app when the error happened. Purpose: fix crashes. Device or other IDs: a random Firebase installation ID. Google may process your IP address to deliver the data. Purpose: tell installations apart and group crashes without identifying you. A crash report never contains your answers, results or name. Driving Theory Test does not read the Android advertising ID, does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking, does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and does not sell your data. Google processes this data on my behalf as a service provider. The data is encrypted in transit. Link to privacy policy of third party service providers used by the app Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy Question updates and videos The app can download new releases of the question database and the explanatory videos from the public GitHub repository slaviboy/bulgarian-driving-exam-database. Your device requests these files from GitHub, which can see your IP address and the request time under its own privacy policy. I do not add any identifier of yours to the request, and the downloaded files are checked before they are used. Links to the official exam materials (public-eis.rta.government.bg) and to GitHub open in your browser only when you tap them, and then the privacy policy of that website applies. GitHub: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement Permissions Driving Theory Test Asks For Internet: download new question releases and videos, and send optional crash reports. The app asks for no other permissions. Sharing I do not sell or rent data and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The only parties that receive data are Google (Firebase Crashlytics) and GitHub (downloads), as described above. I may disclose information if the law requires it. Retention and Your Choices On-device content stays until you delete it or uninstall the app; uninstalling removes it all. Crash data is kept by Firebase for the period set by Google's defaults, after which it is deleted automatically. You can switch off crash reports at any time in Settings > Privacy. Because the data is tied to a random installation ID and not to your identity, I cannot look up one person's data; if you want your installation's data removed, contact me and describe the request. Security Data in transit is sent over HTTPS. Your progress is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and I cannot guarantee its absolute security.

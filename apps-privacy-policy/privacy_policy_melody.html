Privacy Policy Stanislav Georgiev ("I", "me") built the Melody app (com.slaviboy.melody) as a paid app. This SERVICE is provided by me and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform visitors regarding my policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information if anyone decided to use my Service. In short: your songs, recordings and settings stay on your device, and I do not run any account system or server that stores your personal data. The only data that leaves your device is optional usage and crash data, and the downloads of sound packs. If you choose to use my Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy. Information That Stays on Your Device Your music. Songs, takes, recordings, loops, imported files and your settings are stored in the app's private storage. I never receive them. Microphone. If you allow the microphone permission, Melody uses it only while you record, monitor an input, tune with the Amp or turn a whistle or hum into notes. The audio is processed on your device and is not uploaded. Files you choose. Melody opens or saves files (audio, MIDI, MusicXML, .melody projects) only through the system file picker or share sheet, and only the ones you pick or share. Where a file goes after you share it (another app, email, cloud storage) is up to you and that app. Bluetooth and USB MIDI. If you connect a MIDI keyboard or controller, Melody talks to that device to receive notes. Bluetooth permissions are used only for finding and connecting MIDI devices; Melody does not use them to determine your location. On Android 11 and older, Android itself requires the location permission to scan for Bluetooth devices; Melody uses it only for that scan and never reads or stores your location. Information That Leaves Your Device Usage and crash data (optional) Release builds of Melody use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services of Google LLC, to learn which features are used and to find and fix crashes. This is on by default and you can turn it off at any time in Melody > App Settings > Share Usage Data. Turning it off stops collection, deletes crash reports not yet sent and resets the analytics data kept on your device. The data collected is: App activity: which screens, instruments, sounds and features you open (built-in names and choices only, never text you type, names you give songs or anything you record). Purpose: analytics, to improve the app. App info and performance: crash logs and diagnostics such as the app version, Android version, device model, state of the audio engine and whether headphones are connected. Purpose: fix crashes. Device or other IDs: a random Firebase installation (app instance) ID. Google may process your IP address to deliver the data. Purpose: tell installations apart and count users without identifying you. Melody does not read the Android advertising ID, does not use advertising or ad personalisation, does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and does not sell your data. Google processes this data on my behalf as a service provider. The data is encrypted in transit. Link to privacy policy of third party service providers used by the app Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy Sound pack downloads Instrument sounds and some app data are downloaded the first time you use them. Your device requests the files from public hosting (GitHub and GitLab). Like any web request, the host can see your IP address and the request time under its own privacy policy. I do not add any identifier of yours to these requests. Likewise, when you open the Open-Source Licences screen, the licence texts are loaded online. GitHub: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement GitLab: https://about.gitlab.com/privacy/ Permissions Melody Asks For Internet and network state: download sound packs, show whether a failed download is due to being offline, send optional usage and crash data. Microphone: recording, input monitoring, tuner and whistle-to-melody. Requested only when you use these. Bluetooth (scan and connect): connect Bluetooth LE MIDI devices. Sharing I do not sell or rent data and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The only parties that receive data are Google (Firebase) and the hosts of the sound packs, as described above. I may disclose information if the law requires it. Retention and Your Choices On-device content stays until you delete it or uninstall the app; uninstalling removes it all. Usage and crash data is kept by Firebase for the periods set in the Firebase console (Google's defaults), after which it is deleted automatically. You can switch off Share Usage Data at any time. Because the data is tied to a random installation ID and not to your identity, I cannot look up one person's data; if you want your installation's data removed, contact me and describe the request. You can revoke the microphone and Bluetooth permissions in Android settings at any time. Security Data in transit is sent over HTTPS. Your songs and recordings are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and I cannot guarantee its absolute security. Children's Privacy These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. The app asks for no account or personal details. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact me so that I will be able to do necessary actions.

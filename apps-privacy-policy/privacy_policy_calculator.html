Information That Leaves Your Device Crash reports (optional) Release builds of Calculator use Firebase Crashlytics, a service of Google LLC, to find and fix crashes. This is on by default and you can turn it off at any time in Calculator > Settings > Privacy > Crash reports. Debug builds never send reports. The data collected is: App info and performance: crash logs and diagnostics such as the app version, Android version, device model and the state of the app when the error happened. Purpose: fix crashes. Device or other IDs: a random Firebase installation ID. Google may process your IP address to deliver the data. Purpose: tell installations apart and group crashes without identifying you. A crash report does not contain the numbers, expressions or results you enter, or your name or contact details. Calculator does not read the Android advertising ID, does not use advertising or ad personalisation, does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and does not sell your data. Google processes this data on my behalf as a service provider. The data is encrypted in transit.
